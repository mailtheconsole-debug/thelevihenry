About Levi Henry

I spent 12 years in music before I ever touched a sales pipeline.

The pivot looked sudden from the outside. The foundation wasn't new.

As a mixing engineer, producer, songwriter, and artist, I served clients in Nigeria and internationally, working with more than 1,000 artists over the years. But it wasn't just the technical work — I trained producers coming up in the craft, and taught others how the music business actually works: how to build a career, price your work, and turn talent into something sustainable.

Then I pivoted directly into sales — working with international million and billion-dollar companies. That's where I learned how successful businesses are actually built: the technology solutions that power them, how to identify the right tools for a given problem, how to build and train a sales team from the ground up, how to generate leads through inbound and outbound, and how to build and manage a pipeline that consistently turns into revenue. I still perform and release music under the name Halo Mr.

The pivot looked sudden from the outside, but the foundation wasn't new.

Teaching producers how to build a career, teaching artists how the business side works — that's the same work I do now, just applied to a different room. Helping people build the skills that make them valuable, and helping businesses build the systems that turn that value into revenue.

Levi Henry Group is where that experience became a business.

Roots

Built in Nigeria. Sharpened internationally.

The work started at home — in studios across Nigeria, with artists chasing something real — and grew through rooms with international companies operating at a scale most never see up close. Both taught the same lesson: talent is common, but the systems that turn talent into income are rare.

That's the perspective I bring to every engagement — the craftsman's respect for the work, and the operator's respect for what actually produces revenue.

Today

Levi Henry Group today

Consulting, education, and technology — under one roof.

Levi Henry Group is a consulting, education, and technology services company helping individuals build high-income skills and helping small businesses implement the systems they need to grow revenue. We work across three core areas.

Consulting. Hands-on guidance for individuals and small businesses looking to build or scale sales development, marketing, and revenue operations, drawing on 12+ years of experience across sales leadership and channel partnerships.

Education. Practical training and resources that help people develop high-income skills — from sales development and outbound prospecting to marketing and paid acquisition — so they can build sustainable income streams on their own terms.

Technology Services. End-to-end build support for the infrastructure that makes revenue systems actually work: websites, funnels, payment integration, and the technical backbone behind client acquisition and sales.

Strategy, teaching, and technical execution — brought together under one roof.
