Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 August 2026

This policy explains what information Levi Henry Group handles and how. It is written in plain terms; if anything is unclear, contact us at hello@thelevihenry.com.

1. Who we are

Levi Henry Group (“we”, “us”, “our”) is a consulting, education, and technology services company operated by Levi Henry. This policy applies to thelevihenry.com and to the services and communications we provide through it. We are based in Nigeria and work with clients internationally.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact details — such as your name and email address when you use our contact form, email us, or book a call.
  • Message content — anything you choose to tell us about yourself, your goals, or your business.
  • Purchase and enrolment details — if you buy, pre-order, or enrol in a product or program, we (or our payment/course providers) collect the information needed to fulfil it.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical data — like your IP address, browser type, device, and pages viewed, recorded in standard server and content-delivery logs to keep the site secure and working.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide the consulting, education, or technology services you request.
  • To deliver products, courses, and pre-orders you sign up for.
  • To operate, secure, and improve our website.
  • To send you information you have asked for, and to communicate about work in progress.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not send marketing email to people who have not asked to hear from us.

4. Cookies & analytics

Our site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. If we add analytics in the future to understand how the site is used, we will update this policy. Note that fonts are served by Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of delivering those files.

5. How we share information

We share information only with the service providers that help us run the site and communicate with you, and only as needed. These currently include:

  • Netlify — website hosting and delivery.
  • Cloudflare — DNS and email routing (email sent to our domain is forwarded through Cloudflare to our mailbox).
  • Google — email (we read mail in Google’s mail service) and web fonts.
  • Payment or course-hosting providers, if and when you make a purchase or enrol.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights.

6. Data retention

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes above — for example, to maintain our correspondence, deliver a service, or meet legal requirements — and then delete or anonymise it.

7. Security

We use reputable providers and reasonable measures (including HTTPS across the site) to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. To make a request, email hello@thelevihenry.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

9. Third-party links

Our site links to external sites (for example, portfolio projects). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites; please review their policies.

10. Children’s privacy

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “last updated” date above. Significant changes will be made clear on this page.

12. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information? Email hello@thelevihenry.com.
