Two careers, one skill.

For 12 years, I worked as a mixing engineer, producer, songwriter, and artist — serving clients across Nigeria and internationally, and working alongside more than 1,000 artists along the way. Beyond the studio, I trained producers on their craft and taught others how to actually build a career and business in music.

From there, I pivoted directly into sales — working with international million and billion-dollar companies, where I learned how successful businesses actually run: the tech solutions they rely on, how to identify the right tools for the job, how to build and train sales teams, how to generate leads through both inbound and outbound, and how to build and manage a pipeline all the way to revenue.

Levi Henry Group exists to build the people and systems that produce revenue.

Whether you're building yourself or building a business.
