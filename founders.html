For Founders

You built the platform. Now build the go-to-market.

You've built something that solves a real business problem in a genuinely new way. But building it is only the first step — the harder question is how it reaches its first real users.

What it covers

From “built” to “bought.”

Innovator or disruptor, I've seen some seriously solid platforms. The gap is almost never the product — it's go-to-market. How do you pitch businesses or consumers to actually use it? How do you land your first paying users? That's what we build together, in the order that gets you there.

  • ICP & positioningDefine exactly who you're selling to and why they buy, so every message and demo lands with the right person.
  • Go-to-market strategyChannel selection, pricing model, and the sequence for landing your first paying customers.
  • Your first sales motionThe process, cadence, and scripts a non-sales founder (or your first hire) can actually run.

Who it's for

Early-stage startup founders, technical or product-first, who've built a platform but have no go-to-market motion yet: no ideal customer, no pricing or positioning, no first sales process. If you can build it but haven't sold it, this is for you.

The difference

Not the same as sales consulting.

My Sales & Revenue work is for businesses that already have something selling and need a real process behind it. GTM consulting starts a step earlier: it's for founders taking a platform to market for the first time.
